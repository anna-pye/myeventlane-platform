# MyEventLane Product Roadmap

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Status | Directional governance; not a delivery promise |
| Highest authority | [Organiser Manifesto](./00-organiser-manifesto.md) |
| Review cadence | Quarterly, or after material evidence |

## How to use this roadmap

The roadmap sequences outcomes, not predetermined features. “Now”, “Next”, “Later” and “Future” express confidence and dependency, not fixed dates. Movement between horizons requires evidence, capacity, risk review and Product Owner approval.

Every initiative must preserve confidence, community and momentum.

## Now

### Discovery and research

**Why:** Establish what organisers and attendees are trying to achieve, where they hesitate and which failures create support demand.

**Manifesto support:** Builds with communities rather than making assumptions; starts with the human outcome.

### Vendor Studio

**Why:** Make one event, one coherent workspace and one clear next step real across draft, launch and live states.

**Manifesto support:** Turns complexity into guidance, preserves progress and prevents competing workflows.

### Customer experience

**Why:** Make event understanding, trust, booking, confirmation and ticket access coherent for attendees.

**Manifesto support:** Supports the complete journey and earns trust continuously.

### Checkout

**Why:** Provide transparent prices, fees, payment states and recovery for both organiser and attendee.

**Manifesto support:** Shows consequences before commitment and treats payment integrity as a product responsibility.

### Operations

**Why:** Establish support, moderation, incident and release practices capable of protecting people, money and live events.

**Manifesto support:** Supports real conditions and treats trust as something earned.

## Next

### Public discovery

**Why:** Help people find relevant events through clear search, place, date and category signals without turning discovery into a paid popularity contest.

**Manifesto support:** Begins with community and makes the attendee's next step visible.

### Marketing

**Why:** Help organisers communicate, share and promote an event without leaving the event lifecycle or using manipulative urgency.

**Manifesto support:** Invites organisers forward and connects publishing to attendance.

### Analytics

**Why:** Give organisers understandable evidence about reach, booking and attendance while minimising data collection.

**Manifesto support:** Measures outcomes rather than activity and helps organisers learn.

### Community

**Why:** Strengthen trust, belonging, repeat participation and safe local discovery.

**Manifesto support:** Treats community as the reason the product exists, not a segment.

## Later

### Mobile capability

**Why:** Improve high-value on-the-go and event-day tasks after responsive core journeys are dependable.

**Manifesto support:** Designs for real conditions and makes mobile a foundation rather than a desktop reduction.

### Responsible AI

**Why:** Assist organisers with drafting, checking, explanation and prioritisation where assistance demonstrably reduces effort.

**Manifesto support:** Helps technology disappear while retaining judgement, consent and authorship.

### Mature analytics

**Why:** Support comparisons, learning and forecasting only after core data is reliable and understandable.

**Manifesto support:** Makes completed events lead naturally to learning and future events.

### Expanded organiser operations

**Why:** Support teams, repeat programmes and more complex event-day responsibility without burdening first-time organisers.

**Manifesto support:** Uses progressive disclosure and supports growing organisers without weakening the community core.

## Future

### Community network

**Why:** Enable trusted connections among organisers, attendees and local communities when safety, consent and moderation are mature.

**Manifesto support:** Helps create belonging without gamifying participation.

### New markets

**Why:** Extend access only where MyEventLane can maintain support, compliance, payment integrity and community understanding.

**Manifesto support:** Growth deepens purpose rather than diluting it.

### New platforms

**Why:** Adopt native or emerging platforms only when they remove proven barriers in core journeys.

**Manifesto support:** Technology serves the journey and does not become the product.

## Initiative entry criteria

Before an initiative enters “Now”, it must have:

- a defined user and outcome;
- evidence of the problem;
- explicit Manifesto alignment;
- known dependencies and material risks;
- accessibility, privacy, security and mobile considerations;
- a success measure; and
- a clear accountable owner.

## Initiative exit criteria

An initiative is complete only when the intended outcome is observed at an agreed level, operational ownership exists, documentation is current and unresolved risks are accepted explicitly. Shipping is not the same as succeeding.
